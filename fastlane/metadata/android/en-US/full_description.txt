Tracky is a free, open source, privacy-first expense tracker for Android.

There are no payments, subscriptions, ads, analytics or accounts. Your data stays on your device unless you explicitly export it.

Features:
* Multiple accounts for cash, bank, credit card, assets, liabilities and investments
* Expenses, income, transfers and split transactions
* Hierarchical categories, payees, payment methods and tags
* Receipt and photo attachments
* Search and filters for accounts, dates, categories, payees, tags, amounts and void transactions
* Statistics with selectable accounts and custom date ranges
* Monthly budgets with per-category allocations
* Reusable transaction templates
* CSV export and import
* Encrypted backup and restore

Tracky can restore My Expenses backups, including encrypted .enc files.
